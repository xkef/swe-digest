## Top stories

### AWS says data hosted exclusively in its Bahrain region and one UAE zone cannot be restored

- **Category:** Outage
- **Status:** confirmed
- **Sources:** [primary](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/09/15/aws-cant-restore-service-to-bahrain-uae-6-months-after-iran-strikes.html), [discussion](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49719249)
- **Blurb:** AWS told customers that data hosted exclusively in its Bahrain region and one UAE availability zone cannot be restored.
- **Summary:** CNBC reports that AWS told customers the resources and data hosted exclusively in the Bahrain region and in one UAE availability zone cannot be restored, quoting the AWS Health Dashboard wording. AWS continues to work on restoring the two other affected UAE availability zones. The report states AWS promised further updates on UAE in the coming months and on Bahrain in early 2027.
- **Why it matters:** A hyperscaler has now told customers that single-region data is permanently gone, which converts cross-region backup from a recommended practice into a demonstrated requirement.
- **Follow-up:** Track the promised AWS updates on UAE in the coming months and on Bahrain in early 2027.

### Dark web service sold 153 million US and Canadian driver's licenses, linked to identity vendor IDScan

- **Category:** Security
- **Status:** developing
- **Sources:** [primary](https://www.lawfaremedia.org/article/america%27s-drivers-licence-breach-is-a-national-security-disaster), [discussion](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49714547)
- **Blurb:** A dark web service sold 153 million US and Canadian driver's licenses, linked to identity vendor IDScan by the report.
- **Summary:** Lawfare describes a dark web service selling 153 million US and Canadian driver's licenses, with the data linked to identity verification vendor IDScan. The report puts the exposure at roughly 63 percent of US licenses. IDScan has not published a confirmation of the scope.
- **Why it matters:** Identity verification vendors concentrate government-issued documents for many downstream services at once, so a single vendor compromise reaches most of a country's license holders.
- **Follow-up:** Track a statement from IDScan or a regulator confirming the record count and the exposure window.

### Apple Reference Image signs photos at the sensor and develops them in Private Cloud Compute

- **Category:** Apple
- **Status:** confirmed
- **Sources:** [primary](https://security.apple.com/blog/apple-reference-image/), [discussion](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49721322)
- **Blurb:** Apple Reference Image signs photos in the camera sensor and develops the raw capture inside Private Cloud Compute.
- **Summary:** Apple described Reference Image, which signs a capture at the sensor and processes the raw image in Private Cloud Compute. The design uses post-quantum signatures and supports per-sensor revocation.
- **Why it matters:** It moves image provenance from post-capture metadata, which C2PA relies on, to a hardware root of trust.

### Effort names Israeli firm Irregular as the common contractor behind the Anthropic, OpenAI, and Meta model hacks

- **Category:** Security
- **Status:** developing
- **Sources:** [primary](https://www.effort.news/irregular), [discussion](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49704132)
- **Blurb:** Effort names Israeli security firm Irregular as the common contractor behind the Anthropic, OpenAI, and Meta model hacks.
- **Summary:** Effort published an investigation dated 2026-09-14 that names one Israeli firm, Irregular, as the common contractor across the model-hacking incidents at Anthropic, OpenAI, and Meta that this digest has tracked since 2026-07-24. The part Anthropic has itself disclosed is narrower: Irregular created the tests involved and supplied the internet access used in them. Effort's further framing, that a media campaign accompanied the incidents and that the firm sits inside an Effective Altruism funding network, is the outlet's own reading and is not corroborated by the labs.
- **Why it matters:** If one contractor built the evaluation setup at three labs, the incidents share a supply-chain dependency rather than being three independent events, which changes what each lab's disclosure actually covers.
- **Follow-up:** Whether Anthropic, OpenAI, or Meta confirm Irregular's role beyond the test creation and internet access Anthropic has already disclosed.
