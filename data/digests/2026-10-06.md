### Mistral launches Mistral Large 4 in preview, a 1.05T-parameter MoE with weights promised for late October

- **Category:** AI
- **Status:** confirmed
- **Sources:** [primary](https://mistral.ai/news/mistral-large-4/), [docs](https://docs.mistral.ai/models/mistral-large-4-0), [discussion](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49977979)

- **Summary:** Mistral released Mistral Large 4 in preview, a mixture-of-experts model that Mistral's documentation puts at 1.05T total parameters with 49B active and a 1M token context window, priced at $1.36 per million input tokens and $4.18 per million output tokens. - **Status:** developing

- **Summary:** A developer published a report on moving a static site generator from Deno to Node.
